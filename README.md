## 🇨🇳 中文

可提取、下载、解析、理解在线视频或本地视频文件。支持快手、B 站、AcFun、芒果 TV、梨视频、微博、X 平台、Facebook、Instagram、TikTok、Twitch、YouTube 等视频平台，也支持视频直链和本地视频。可提取的内容包含音频、字幕、逐字稿、文案、脚本、总结、章节、关键词与时间轴；也能理解画面、人物、物体、动作、界面等视觉元素，并通过抽帧给出证据截图。第一步按你的意图路由到下载、文本提取还是画面理解，再走对应链路。遇到无法访问的平台或分享口令时会明确说明限制，不瞎猜。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 输入视频链接或分享文本 → 保存 MP4 并返回来源平台、实际文件路径与结构化字段
- 提取音频、字幕、逐字稿、文案、脚本、总结、章节、关键词与时间轴
- 判断某人物、某物体是否出现时，先匹配逐字稿时间窗再抽帧取证
- 支持本地视频文件，离线环境照常做转写与画面分析
- 先做外网平台运行环境检查，访问不了就提示切换本地电脑模式
- 遇到分享口令而非可访问链接时明确拒绝解析，不联网瞎猜

## 🇬🇧 English

Extracts, downloads, parses, and understands online or local video. It supports Kuaishou, Bilibili, AcFun, Mango TV, Pear Video, Weibo, X, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Twitch, YouTube, direct video URLs, and local files. You can pull audio, subtitles, transcripts, copy, scripts, summaries, chapters, keywords, and timelines, or ask what appears on screen and get frame-level evidence for people, objects, actions, and interfaces. The first step routes your intent to download, text extraction, or visual understanding. Unreachable platforms and share-password text fail with a clear message instead of a guess.

### ✨ What it does

- Paste a link or share text → saved MP4 plus source platform, actual path, and structured metadata
- Extracts audio, subtitles, transcripts, copy, scripts, summaries, chapters, keywords, and timelines
- Answers presence questions by matching transcript time windows first, then pulling frames as evidence
- Handles local files too, so transcription and visual analysis still work offline
- Runs an external-network precheck and says to switch to local mode when a platform is unreachable
- Refuses share-password text instead of guessing at a link
